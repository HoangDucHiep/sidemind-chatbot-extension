Transformer models exhibit a fascinating regularity during inference: certain attention heads converge on a small set of tokens regardless of context. Recent work has shown these "attention sinks" provide numerical stability that would otherwise require additional normalization…

Three distinct mechanisms appear to drive this behavior. First, the residual stream's magnitude grows monotonically with depth, creating drift.
